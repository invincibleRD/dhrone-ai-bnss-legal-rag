SAMPLE PROCEDURAL CLAUSES (toy document, for demo/testing only)

Clause 1. Filing of a Complaint. Any aggrieved person may file a written
complaint before the designated authority within thirty days of the event
giving rise to the complaint, unless a longer period is expressly allowed.

Clause 2. Acknowledgement. Upon receipt of a complaint, the authority shall
issue a written acknowledgement to the complainant within seven working days.

Clause 3. Preliminary Review. The authority shall conduct a preliminary
review to determine whether the complaint discloses a matter requiring
further inquiry, and shall record reasons for its decision.

Clause 4. Notice to Respondent. Where further inquiry is warranted, notice
shall be issued to the respondent, who shall be given not less than fifteen
days to submit a written response.

Clause 5. Hearing. Both parties shall be given a reasonable opportunity of
being heard before any adverse order is passed against them.

Clause 6. Recording of Evidence. The authority may receive documents and
statements as evidence and shall maintain a record of proceedings.

Clause 7. Final Order. The authority shall pass a final order in writing,
stating the reasons for its decision, within ninety days of the first
hearing, unless an extension is granted for recorded reasons.

Clause 8. Appeal. Any party aggrieved by the final order may prefer an
appeal to the appellate authority within sixty days of receiving the order.

Clause 9. Limitation. No proceeding under these clauses shall be initiated
after the expiry of three years from the date of the alleged act, except
where the delay is condoned for sufficient cause shown.

Clause 10. Confidentiality. The identity of the complainant shall be kept
confidential to the extent possible, unless disclosure is required by law.
